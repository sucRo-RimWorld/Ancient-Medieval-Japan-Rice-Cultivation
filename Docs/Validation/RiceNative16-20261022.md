## Test premises and behavioral assertions

1. **Construction:** rectangle designation of an empty 3×3 field does not instantly finish; the real Vanilla Construction pawn starts the production `AMJR_ConstructPaddy` job, advances saved work, is interrupted, and resumes. A second pawn cannot acquire a simultaneous plot-wide reservation.
2. **Zone behavior:** finished initially unzoned 3×3 field gets one Vanilla `Zone_Growing` (9 cells) and existing Vanilla `Plant_Rice`; an adjoining independent 2×2 plot with a one-cell preexisting potato Growing Zone does not overwrite, delete or expand the existing Zone.
3. **Terrain and intake:** original Soil remains Soil; only the explicitly exposed and supplied Waterworks canal beside the completed plot makes it wet. A Vanilla Bridge foundation over the contact disables intake; removing the cover restores it. Loss/restoration of adjacent moving freshwater dries/rewets it. Independent neighboring plot is not implicitly wet.
4. **Drawing route:** original game map contains `SectionLayer_AMJR_Paddy` before and after reload. This proves runtime registration of the drawing layer, **not** actual pixel-level appearance or artist acceptance.
5. **Persistence:** genuine disk save/load keeps plot IDs, completion states, Vanilla growing Zones, Soil, reconstructed supplied/unsupplied conditions, and a deliberately unfinished plot with the unchanged remaining work amount.

The wet/soil/zone assertions are acceptance of the **binary functional contract**, not validation of a crop growth bonus, economic yield or real-world irrigation rate. The test does not validate every existing partial/multiple-Zone permutation, all blocked terrain messages, all placements, seasons, large farms or arbitrary integrations.
